Why a second opinion?

An experienced, independent view – before you decide

A lease is one of the largest and longest commitments a company makes. For the owner of an office building, letting is decisive for both income and the value of the property. Yet these decisions are often based on one party's assessment.

Just as you would seek a second opinion before any important decision, you get an experienced and independent assessment of your lease – before you sign, renegotiate or change strategy.

The assessment draws on more than 25 years of experience with commercial property, over 1 million square metres of office space let, and advice to more than 100 property companies and asset managers – as well as national and international companies on the tenant side.

Are you a tenant? A second opinion on your lease Is your rent in line with the market? Are the terms reasonable? Is it better to stay – or to move? Tenant advisory Are you a landlord?
